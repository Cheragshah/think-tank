# Positions: Narendra Modi

34 topics. Each stance has a verbatim quote, source id, and date (Mann Ki Baat upload dates and speech dates from `sources.md`; S049 and S050 use the note dates 2024-03-20 and 2026-01-16). Modi is a political leader and national executor, not a business operator, so these map his governance positions onto founder questions. Where he changed his mind, both views sit side by side with dates and the newer one is marked. Topics with no evidence say so.

## Startups and innovation
- **Stance:** A spreading startup culture, especially outside big cities, is the signal of a good future, and innovation is treated as survival, not optional.
- 2021-08-29: "Today, the startup culture is expanding even to smaller cities and I am seeing it as an indication of a bright future." (S001)
- 2023-12-01: "the development of a country which does not give importance to innovation, stops." (S014)
- 2026-01-01: "Today India has turned into the third largest Start-ups ecosystem in the world." (S026)
- **Read:** he frames innovation as the growth engine and measures it by rank, patents, and startup count (S014, S026, S049).

## The startup founder
- **Stance:** The thing that makes a founder is confidence in the future and choosing to create jobs rather than seek them.
- "the genius element within you is what makes the start-up successful." (S049, 2024-03-20)
- "The youth of the country have chosen the path of becoming job creators rather than job seekers." (S049, 2024-03-20)
- "the focus of India's youth is on solving real problems." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- He says the shift from jobs-only to startups is recent and hard-won (S049, S050).

## Raising money and access to capital
- **Stance:** Capital is a gate to the market, so the state must remove collateral as the obstacle through funds and guarantees.
- "without capital, even the best ideas cannot reach the market." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "through the Fund of Funds for Startups, investments of more than ₹25,000 crore have been made." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- He built funds and credit guarantees specifically so that collateral is not the barrier to a good idea (S050). A one lakh crore rupee research and innovation fund was announced separately (S049).

## Self-reliance versus imports (build versus buy)
- **Stance:** Build domestic capability rather than depend on imports for anything critical. This is his build-versus-buy rule, applied to semiconductors, energy, defence, and space.
- "We must systematically increase our own capabilities and securely protect our own interests ourselves," (S032, 2026-08-15)
- "we have to become self-reliant, we have to make the country self-reliant; and the path to that lies only through Swadeshi." (S024, 2025-09-01)
- **Origin story:** "when India was denied the Cryogenic Rocket Technology. But the scientists of India not only developed indigenous technology, but today with the help of it, dozens of satellites are being sent to space simultaneously." (S008, 2022-10-30)
- **Read:** he treats import denial as the trigger that forces indigenous build (S008), and holds that build, not buy, is the only route to a developed nation (S022, S024, S032).

## Manufacturing and quality
- **Stance:** Move from services to making physical products, and make quality the single test.
- "Today, India has become a manufacturing powerhouse." (S017, 2024-09-01)
- "Now the time has come for our startups to focus more on manufacturing." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "We must produce the best quality products in the world." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "Let our only mantra be quality, quality and only quality." (S026, 2026-01-01)
- "there will be no laxity on quality, nor will there be any compromise on quality." (S026, 2026-01-01)

## Competition (global competitiveness)
- **Stance:** Compete by being categorically better and cheaper than what the world offers, and win on innovation and scale rather than protected margins.
- "We will have to categorically give better and cheaper products than what we currently get from the world," (S032, 2026-08-15)
- "durable competitiveness must rest on innovation, efficiency and scale." (S046, 2026-02-01)
- "our ambition should not be limited to partnership alone. We must aim for global leadership." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "Any country that wants to grow at scale and grow quickly cannot do so by remaining only a consumer." (S046, 2026-02-01)
- He adds that a nation must be a producer and a creator, not a consumer or a late adopter (S046, S047).

## AI
- **Stance:** Create AI, don't just consume it, and keep human beings responsible for decisions. AI must reach the last mile, not a narrow elite.
- "No matter what the world does with AI, it will remain incomplete without India." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "India should be among the top three AI superpowers globally" (S047, 2026-07-01)
- "Technology exists to serve humanity, not replace it." (S047, 2026-07-01)
- "the ultimate responsibility for decision-making must always remain with human beings." (S047, 2026-07-01)
- "AI can provide answers quickly. But the responsibility of verifying the truth of those answers must remain with the scientist." (S038, 2026-10-01)

## AI and jobs
- **Stance:** Technology changes the nature of work rather than deleting it, so the response is skilling, not fear.
- "History has shown that work does not disappear due to technology. Its nature changes and new types of jobs are created." (S047, 2026-07-01)
- "Preparation is the best antidote to fear." (S047, 2026-07-01)
- "We will aggressively work to train one crore youth for AI skills so our youth has the absolute capability to lead in the world of AI," (S032, 2026-08-15)

## Digital delivery and public infrastructure
- **Stance:** Build digital rails as a public good so that a full rupee reaches the last person with no leakage, and democratize technology so the have-nots disappear.
- "Today, when one rupee leaves Delhi, the entire one rupee reaches the bank account of every beneficiary." (S037, 2026-10-01)
- "I introduced direct benefit transfer, ensuring that every rupee sent from Delhi reached the rightful person without leakage." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "we built digital infrastructure as a public good, not a proprietary platform." (S047, 2026-07-01)
- "Bharat has democratized technology, so the theory of haves and have-nots cannot thrive here." (S049, 2024-03-20)

## Regulation and compliance (ease of doing business)
- **Stance:** The state's default is to put up barriers, so his entire effort goes into removing them: fewer compliances, fewer laws, decriminalized provisions, self-certification.
- "Initially, there is a tendency within the government to put up barriers, and my entire effort goes into breaking those barriers." (S049, 2024-03-20)
- "Under the Jan Vishwas Act, more than 180 provisions have been decriminalised." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "I eliminated 40,000 unnecessary compliances, and removed nearly 1500 outdated laws that served no purpose." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "We envisage the state as an enabler, and we trust the citizens." (S046, 2026-02-01)
- "We saved your time so that you could focus on innovation, and not waste it in litigation." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- On drones he shows the cost of over-regulation: "The technology that should have been seen as an opportunity was seen as a crisis." (S002, 2021-10-24)

## Talent, skilling and hiring
- **Stance:** Youth power is the base of a capable nation, and a student mindset must never die. Exams cannot be the whole measure of a person.
- "the building of youth power! And, through youth power, the creation of an empowered and capable India." (S035, 2026-10-01)
- "Student power is the basis of making India powerful." (S008, 2022-10-30)
- "one should never let the student within them die. Learning should never stop." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "Exams are valuable for assessing knowledge in a specific domain, but they can't become the sole measure of someone's overall potential." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- He has no recorded position on hiring pace or firing; his hiring instinct is to skill at national scale, not to hire selectively (inferred from S032, S047).

## Women-led development
- **Stance:** Women power is his named development lever, and women-led startups and directors are a measurable proof point he returns to constantly.
- "we have to strengthen Women Led Development as a national character. Where the might of women power is added, the impossible can also be made possible." (S012, 2023-08-01)
- "in more than 45 percent of recognized startups, there is at least one woman director or partner." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "It is my conviction that women will play the most important role in creating Viksit Bharat." (S046, 2026-02-01)

## Brand (Made in India / Vocal for Local)
- **Stance:** A product's origin is the brand. Buying and gifting Indian-made goods is a standing instruction, and the end goal is to take local products global.
- "Make 'Vocal for Local' your shopping mantra." (S024, 2025-09-01)
- "Anything you buy, should necessarily be 'Made in India'... Anything you gift that too should be Made In India." (S017, 2024-09-01)
- "we don't just buy goods; we bring home hope to a family, honor the hard work of an artisan, and give wings to the dreams of a young entrepreneur." (S024, 2025-09-01)
- "The 'One District, One Product' campaign aims to promote these very specialties." (S030, 2026-09-01)
- "when I say Vocal for Local and now Local for Global" (S011, 2023-04-30)

## Pricing, subsidy and absorbing cost
- **Stance:** When costs spike, the state absorbs the shock rather than passing it to the poor. He absorbs fertiliser and fuel costs and keeps essentials cheap.
- He absorbed fertiliser costs so that urea that sells globally near Rs 3,000 stays under Rs 300, and absorbed about Rs 43 of a Rs 50 per litre diesel hike, raising prices only about Rs 7 and bearing roughly Rs 14,000 crore a month (S036, 2026-11-01, paraphrased from the Hindi source).
- He holds that farmers must not carry the burden of farming and that policy and intent must be clean (S036). In Gujarat he framed the rule as shielding farmers from global shocks by absorbing the cost rather than passing it on (S040).
- **Read:** absorb cost for the vulnerable, not as a discount tactic, and pair it with delivery (S036, S040).

## Debt and fiscal discipline
- **No recorded position** on startup debt financing. His fiscal instinct is public capital expenditure as the growth engine (about Rs 12.2 lakh crore, five times 2013) and protecting households from cost shocks, rather than household leverage (S046, 2026-02-01). He never counsels founders on taking on debt (inferred from S046).

## Infrastructure
- **Stance:** Infrastructure is built by before-and-after targets (metro from 248 km to about 1,200 km, from 5 to 26 cities) and saves time, which raises productivity.
- "India has taken this major step in the aircraft maintenance, repair, and overhaul sector." (S025, 2025-11-01)
- He holds that when a city's time is saved, its productivity rises, which benefits the economy and the poor and middle class (S039, 2026-11-01, paraphrased). Multimodal logistics is one of his seven named growth streams (S032).

## Timing and sequencing
- **Stance:** Right decisions at the right time, taken early and before they are fashionable, are the foundation. Institutions must change with the century.
- "Bharat has taken the right decisions at the right time and initiated work on start-ups at the right moment." (S049, 2024-03-20)
- "If the century changes and we do not change, then our capacity to bring change also weakens," (S033, 2026-10-01)
- He frames the budget as a sequenced roadmap with timelines rather than an ad hoc ledger (S046, 2026-02-01, paraphrased).

## Focus versus diversification
- **Stance:** One overriding goal, many synchronized work streams. He is a diversifier who insists everything points at a single north star.
- "With the tremendous capability and power of that seven-stream strategy, we will aggressively give the country a new height and a new speed," (S032, 2026-08-15)
- The one goal is a developed India by 2047; the seven streams are manufacturing, agriculture, technology, logistics, defence, green and blue economy, and soft power (S032). He collapses policy into one mantra, one path, one goal (S023).

## Side projects
- **No recorded position** on founders running side projects. The nearest is his mentorship instruction that successful founders adopt labs and new founders, which is about giving time and guidance rather than money (S049, 2024-03-20).

## Mentorship (pay it forward)
- **Stance:** Someone once held your hand, so hold someone else's. Mentorship is time and guidance, not just capital.
- "Someone was there who held your hand; you should also hold someone else's hand." (S049, 2024-03-20)

## Patents and intellectual property
- **Stance:** File patents early and in parallel with building, because someone else can take the lead.
- "My earnest request is to start that work alongside. Because the world is changing so rapidly today, you never know who might take the lead." (S049, 2024-03-20)
- He also presses founders directly: "Have you obtained a patent?" (S049, 2024-03-20)

## Culture
- **Stance:** Culture means pride in your roots and your product, and collective ownership produces results. A country progresses by taking pride in its culture.
- "a country can progress only by taking pride in her culture." (S016, 2024-07-01)
- "when we stay connected to our roots, no matter how big the storm, it cannot uproot us." (S020, 2025-03-01)
- "when the sense of ownership strengthens in the society, amazing results happen." (S021, 2025-05-01)

## Metrics and targets
- **Stance:** Make the goal numeric and dated so fulfillment cannot remain in doubt, and state confidence in the capability.
- "When so many massive accomplishments are before us, the resolution to become a Viksit Bharat can never remain unfulfilled," (S032, 2026-08-15)
- "This target is somewhat challenging, but I have full confidence in the capabilities of my country." (S034, 2026-11-01)
- He attaches a number to nearly every claim and sets time-boxed outcomes (for example, what prototype or product in three years) (S038, inferred from S032, S034).

## Risk-taking and owning outcomes
- **Stance:** Take the necessary risk and own the outcome instead of shifting blame. Ownership makes the team commit.
- "If something goes wrong, I don't ship blame to others. I stand up, take responsibility, and own the outcome." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "If there is a loss, it will be mine; but if there is a gain, then millions of families in my country will benefit." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "the work which is necessary for the nation must be done by someone, and someone must take the risk." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "earlier, risk-taking was discouraged in the country, but today risk-taking has become mainstream." (S050, 2026-01-16)

## Failure and pivoting
- **Stance:** Failure is the raw material of the next confidence. On the day of a failure, begin scripting the next success.
- "when Chandrayaan-3 successfully landed, it was not just the success of a mission. It was the success of the new confidence built after coming out of failure." (S025, 2025-11-01)
- "failure did not stop them. That very day they began scripting the story of Chandrayaan-3's success." (S025, 2025-11-01)
- "if one launch doesn't work, you swiftly move on to the next." (S049, 2024-03-20)

## Selling the company
- **No recorded position.** The nearest is his ambition that India not stop at partnership: "our ambition should not be limited to partnership alone. We must aim for global leadership." (S050, 2026-01-16). He never addresses an exit or acquisition.

## Discounting
- **No recorded position** on discounting as a sales tactic. His competitiveness frame is to be better and cheaper at once, not to cut price to survive (S032, 2026-08-15). Absorbing cost is reserved for protecting the poor, not for acquiring customers (S036, S040).

## Remote work
- **No recorded position** on remote or distributed teams. He builds digital delivery rails (UPI, telemedicine) but never advises founders on where teams should sit (inferred from S005, S010).

## Work hours and hard work
- **Stance:** There are no shortcuts, and hard work is a standing personal commitment. Discipline, including fasting, sharpens thinking.
- "There are no shortcuts in life." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "I will never fall behind in hard work for my country." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "I am more focused on productivity than power." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "fasting can greatly accelerate the thinking process, and can give a fresh perspective. You start thinking outside the box." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- He has no recorded position on burnout or rest; his frame is discipline and productivity, not time off (inferred from S048).

## The core decision test
- **Stance:** Put the country first and run every decision through the poorest person's face. Learn like a student and argue both sides before committing.
- "My country first. I always question if what I'm doing harms my nation in any way." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "think of the poorest person's face. Remember them and ask yourself, Will this help them? Then your decision will be right." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "I approach them like a student and ask, Can you clarify this? How does it work? Then what happens next and how?" (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "I deliberately play devil's advocate and ask challenging questions." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- His named mantra is Rashtra Pratham, Nation First, with no greater mantra than it (S014, 2023-12-01, paraphrased).

## Public trust and reputation
- **Stance:** Trust is the cornerstone, and it is built by equal treatment and delivery to the last person.
- "trust is the cornerstone of my governance model." (S048, 2025-03-16)
- "This shows the trust of every Indian in the system; shows trust in science and in scientists." (S003, 2021-12-26)

## Politics and regulatory capital
- **Stance:** Do the necessary work even at political cost, own the outcome, and keep the platform above partisan politics.
- "the work which is necessary for the nation must be done by someone, and someone must take the risk." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- "adhering to political decorum, 'Mann Ki Baat' will not be broadcast for the next three months in these days of Lok Sabha elections." (S015, 2024-02-01)
- He distinguishes real opposition from obstruction and refuses to treat obstruction as legitimate politics (S043, 2026-10-31, paraphrased).

## Changed mind: novice to executor
- **Then (2001):** "At that time, I knew nothing about government offices, government functioning, or files." (S040, 2026-10-01) He also says he had "absolutely no prior experience with government" when he became chief minister (S048, 2025-03-16).
- **Now:** "You taught me everything, and I simply went and implemented it." (S040, 2026-10-01) He frames his execution record as learned on the ground from the people, not from prior office.
- **Read:** his execution playbook is hard-won, not inherited, and he treats first-hand ground reality (staying overnight in most districts) as his primary decision input (S048).

## Changed mind: risk and quality culture
- **Then:** risk-taking was discouraged in the country (S050, 2026-01-16).
- **Now:** "earlier, risk-taking was discouraged in the country, but today risk-taking has become mainstream." (S050, 2026-01-16)
- **Then:** "The era of 'it just happens... it works... it will go through somehow' is over." (S026, 2026-01-01)
- **Now:** "Let our only mantra be quality, quality and only quality." (S026, 2026-01-01)
- **Read:** he describes a national shift from mediocrity and risk-aversion to excellence and mainstream risk, and treats that shift as settled (S026, S050).
