# Principles: Narendra Modi

24 decision rules. Sources is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Run every hard decision through the poorest person's face and ask whether it reaches the last person.** "The resolution of a Viksit Bharat will only be truly fulfilled when massive development successfully reaches the very last person," (S032) Sources: 5 (S032, S037, S039, S047, S048). Confidence: high.

2. **Put the mission first and ask whether the move harms it before asking what you gain.** "My country first. I always question if what I'm doing harms my nation in any way." (S048) Sources: 4 (S014, S040, S042, S048). Confidence: high.

3. **Leak-proof the pipeline so the full value reaches the beneficiary, with no middleman in between.** "I introduced direct benefit transfer, ensuring that every rupee sent from Delhi reached the rightful person without leakage." (S048) Sources: 3 (S037, S039, S048). Confidence: high.

4. **Take the risk if it is right, and own the outcome instead of shifting blame.** "If something goes wrong, I don't ship blame to others. I stand up, take responsibility, and own the outcome." (S048) "If there is a loss, it will be mine; but if there is a gain, then millions of families in my country will benefit." (S050) Sources: 2 (S048, S050). Confidence: medium.

5. **There are no shortcuts.** "Shortcut will cut you short." (S048) Sources: 1 (S048). Confidence: low.

6. **Make the goal numeric and dated, and commit to delivering it on time or early.** "We said: We will do it!" (S034) "Until the country is completely free from Naxalism and Maoist terror, we will not rest." (S043) Sources: 3 (S032, S034, S043). Confidence: high.

7. **Never let a campaign's momentum halt; keep moving and keep replenishing it.** "should this momentum be allowed to subside, to halt now? No...not at all." (S001) Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S006, S011). Confidence: high.

8. **Build your own capability rather than depend on a cheap import.** "We must systematically increase our own capabilities and securely protect our own interests ourselves," (S032) Sources: 4 (S032, S034, S038, S047). Confidence: high.

9. **Buy local so your spending reaches the people around you rather than a distant supplier.** "If you buy local, then your festival will also be illuminated and the house of a poor brother or sister, an artisan, or a weaver will also be lit up." (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S004, S013, S024). Confidence: high.

10. **In a crisis, act on verified facts and continuous information, not rumours.** "Trust only the continuous information provided by the government and take action based only on that." (S027) Sources: 2 (S027, S040). Confidence: medium.

11. **Lead and create technology rather than consume and catch up.** "India must embrace the technologies of the future, innovate boldly, and lead the world in creating them." (S038) Sources: 3 (S038, S046, S047). Confidence: high.

12. **Make quality the only acceptable standard, with no laxity and no compromise.** "Let our only mantra be quality, quality and only quality." (S026) Sources: 4 (S017, S026, S032, S046). Confidence: high.

13. **Do not give up after a failure; begin scripting the next attempt the same day.** "failure did not stop them. That very day they began scripting the story of Chandrayaan-3's success." (S025) Sources: 1 (S025). Confidence: low.

14. **Never let the student in you die; keep learning and asking.** "one should never let the student within them die. Learning should never stop." (S048) Sources: 1 (S048). Confidence: low.

15. **Dream of doing something rather than becoming something.** "instead of dreaming about getting and becoming, dream of doing something." (S048) Sources: 1 (S048). Confidence: low.

16. **Keep a human being accountable for the decision; technology serves people, not the reverse.** "Technology exists to serve humanity, not replace it." (S047) "the ultimate responsibility for decision-making must always remain with human beings." (S047) Sources: 2 (S038, S047). Confidence: medium.

17. **Remove barriers and compliances so the doer's time goes to the work, not to litigation.** "We saved your time so that you could focus on innovation, and not waste it in litigation." (S050) Sources: 5 (S002, S009, S048, S049, S050). Confidence: high.

18. **Empower the person rather than only give them things.** "when you empower the poor, the poor gain the courage to lift themselves out of poverty" (S039) Sources: 2 (S033, S039). Confidence: medium.

19. **Keep clean and care for the basics, because cleanliness compounds into capability and prosperity.** "where there is cleanliness, there is health, where there is health, there is capability, and where there is capability, there is prosperity." (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S017, S022, S031). Confidence: high.

20. **With everyone's effort converged, even the biggest goal is certain.** "the biggest change can be brought about by collective effort." (S011) Sources: 4 (S004, S009, S011, S022). Confidence: high.

21. **Separate genuine criticism from allegations, and keep the critics close.** "There's a big difference between allegations and criticism." (S048) Sources: 1 (S048). Confidence: low.

22. **Change with the century, or lose the power to change anything.** "If the century changes and we do not change, then our capacity to bring change also weakens," (S033) Sources: 2 (S033, S045). Confidence: medium.

23. **Preparation is the best antidote to fear, so invest in skilling early.** "Preparation is the best antidote to fear." (S047) Sources: 1 (S047). Confidence: low.

24. **Prove it in one hard and varied market, then scale it everywhere.** "whatever innovation is tried and tested in Bharat will be successful in every geography and demography in the world." (S049) Sources: 2 (S049, S050). Confidence: medium.
