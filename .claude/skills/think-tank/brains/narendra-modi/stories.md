# Stories: Narendra Modi

16 stories and cases he reuses, with the lesson he draws. Source ids in parentheses.

1. **The 100 million ghost beneficiaries.** He removed about a hundred million fake or duplicate welfare names and shifted to direct benefit transfer, which he says saved nearly 3 trillion rupees. Lesson: scrutiny plus technology eliminates leakage and middlemen (S048).

2. **"15 paise reaches" versus the full rupee.** He contrasts a former PM's remark that only 15 paise of a rupee reached the poor with today's direct transfer. "Today, when one rupee leaves Delhi, the entire one rupee reaches the bank account of every beneficiary." Lesson: digital delivery ends leakage and rebuilds trust (S037).

3. **Chandrayaan failure to success.** When Chandrayaan-2 lost contact, scientists began scripting Chandrayaan-3 the same day, and the later landing became proof of recovered confidence. "when Chandrayaan-3 successfully landed, it was not just the success of a mission. It was the success of the new confidence built after coming out of failure." Lesson: treat failure as the raw material of the next attempt (S025).

4. **Opening space to private startups.** A sector that was a closed government monopoly was opened to private enterprise, and within years startups grew from under 50 to over 200, launching their own satellites and rockets. Lesson: deregulation of a closed sector unlocks a whole industry (S006, S019, S022, S029, S048).

5. **Vikram-1, the first private rocket.** The first private-sector launch vehicle flew after the space sector opened. Lesson: opening a sector to private enterprise yields world-visible results (S029).

6. **The Sukhet and Modhera solar villages.** Villages that turned waste into fuel and fertilizer, and Modhera, India's first fully solar village where houses get paid instead of paying an electricity bill. Lesson: local resources and solar energy turn a cost into income and self-reliance (S001, S008, S030).

7. **The startup summit that began half-empty.** When the word "startup" was unknown, his first summit barely filled an auditorium; ten years later the same program fills Bharat Mandapam. Lesson: he started before it was fashionable, and scale compounds when a nation backs its youth (S049, S050).

8. **The mother who called a startup "utter disaster."** A founder's educated mother dismissed a startup as disaster or ruin, and he retells it to mark how far the mindset has shifted from jobs-only to building. Lesson: the social default once fought founders, and that default has changed (S049, S050).

9. **The Bastar Naxal turnaround.** After decades of Maoist violence, the first Diwali free of terror in 40 to 50 years was celebrated, with roads, towers, and schools following. Lesson: sacrifice plus political will ends decades of violence and restores normal life (S042, S043).

10. **Canvas shoes polished with chalk.** As a boy he polished his only pair of white canvas shoes with chalk paste to keep them white. Lesson: resourcefulness and pride in what little you have, without feeling poor (S048).

11. **His father's disciplined footsteps.** His father left at 4:00 AM, and villagers could tell the time by his footsteps. Lesson: discipline is visible and becomes a person's identity (S048).

12. **Fasting discipline and the Obama dinner.** He prepared for fasts with days of Ayurvedic and yoga practice and once joked at a White House dinner, while fasting, that his hot water was dinner. "fasting can greatly accelerate the thinking process, and can give a fresh perspective. You start thinking outside the box." Lesson: discipline can be worn lightly and sharpens the mind (S048).

13. **Namo Drone Didi.** A rural woman trained to fly an agricultural drone is now called "pilot" by her village. Lesson: technology plus a determined woman equals farm modernization and self-respect (S015, S036).

14. **The tailor who went online.** "Darzi Online" was a tailor who treated Covid as opportunity, moved online, and now employs hundreds of women. Lesson: digital rails turn adversity into scale (S007).

15. **Amul's AI for women dairy farmers.** AI reached 36 lakh women dairy farmers in Gujarati. Lesson: AI dissolves divides rather than deepening them (S047).

16. **Mobile phones from importer to maker.** A country that imported almost all its phones a decade ago is now the world's second-largest manufacturer. Lesson: Make in India turns a neglected sector around (S046).
