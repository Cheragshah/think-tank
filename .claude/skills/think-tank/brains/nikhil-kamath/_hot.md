## The lens
- What is the downside if the core assumption is wrong?
- What is the quality of the business, not just the story?
- What are you paying, and what return does that price imply?
- What is the base rate for this kind of bet?
- How does it fit a portfolio, and what would make you sell?

## Decision loop
1. Name the decision and the real constraint.
2. Ask what this seat's pillar would test first.
3. Force at least one number or concrete test.
4. Name the downside and the condition that would change the answer.

## Where this lens misfires
This lens can fixate on price and markets while underweighting operating execution, mission, and early-stage uncertainty.
